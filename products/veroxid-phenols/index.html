VerOxid Phenols

Natural Antioxidant Solution — Harnessing the Power of Polyphenols for Optimal Health

VEROXID Phenols is a dry blend of carefully selected natural extracts rich in polyphenols, designed to provide potent antioxidant protection for animals. What feed ingredients does VerOxid Phenols protect?

VerOxid Phenols protects feed fats, oils, vitamin premixes, and carotenoid pigments from oxidative rancidity during storage and processing.

What is the recommended inclusion rate?

Typical inclusion rates range from 100-500g per tonne of feed, depending on fat content and storage conditions. Higher rates recommended for high-fat formulations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Veroxid Phenols and synthetic antioxidants?

Veroxid Phenols uses a blend of natural phenolic compounds including rosemary extract, tocopherols, and citric acid derivatives, whereas synthetic options like BHT and BHA are petroleum-derived. Natural phenols offer equivalent antioxidant protection with positive consumer perception for products marketed as natural or clean-label.

Is Veroxid Phenols suitable for organic feed production?

Yes, Veroxid Phenols is formulated specifically for organic and natural feed production systems. All ingredients are derived from plant sources and comply with EU Organic Regulation (EU 2018/848) and USDA National Organic Program (NOP) requirements.

How does Veroxid Phenols protect vitamins in premixes?

Veroxid Phenols protects fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) and carotenoids in premixes through dual-action antioxidant activity. It scavenges peroxyl radicals before they can oxidize vitamins, and chelates pro-oxidant metal ions that catalyze oxidative degradation.

What species is Veroxid Phenols recommended for?

Veroxid Phenols is recommended for all livestock species including poultry, ruminants, swine, and aquaculture. It is particularly valued in layer diets where tocopherol deposition in eggs is desired, and in dairy rations where vitamin stability in total mixed rations (TMR) is important.

Can Veroxid Phenols replace BHT/BHA in feed formulations?

Yes, Veroxid Phenols can serve as a direct replacement for synthetic antioxidants in most feed applications. Typical replacement ratio is 1:1 to 1.5:1 (natural phenols to synthetic) depending on fat level and oxidation sensitivity of the diet.
