Frequently Asked Questions — Digestive & Gut Health Feed Additives

What are digestive feed additives and how do they support gut health in animals?

Digestive feed additives are formulated products that support the gastrointestinal function of livestock and poultry. They work by maintaining optimal gut pH, promoting beneficial microbiota, protecting intestinal integrity, and improving nutrient absorption — resulting in better feed conversion and overall animal performance.

How do gut health feed additives differ from standard nutritional supplements?

While standard supplements provide basic nutritional support, gut health feed additives target the intestinal environment specifically. They help maintain gut integrity, support the intestinal microbiome, and protect against pathogens that can compromise digestive function and reduce animal productivity.

What role do digestive feed additives play in maintaining gut integrity in livestock?

Digestive feed additives support gut integrity by strengthening the intestinal lining, promoting the development of villi and crypt structures, and reducing gut permeability. This helps prevent leakage of pathogens and toxins into the bloodstream while optimizing the absorption of nutrients from feed.

Can I combine multiple gut health feed additives in one feeding program?

Yes, many gut health feed additives can be combined in comprehensive feeding programs. For example, acidifiers like VersaAcid Liquid can be used alongside probiotic supports like VersaMos to deliver both pathogen control and microbiome support. Our technical team can advise on compatible combinations for your specific operation.
