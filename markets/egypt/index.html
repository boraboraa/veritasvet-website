Egypt Market

1. Market Overview

Egypt's poultry sector is the largest in North Africa, with annual production exceeding 1.4 billion birds. The industry operates across small-to-medium farms alongside large integrated complexes.

Feed represents 65–75% of total production cost, making feed efficiency and hygiene critical to profitability. Imported corn and soybean meal carry significant mycotoxin risk given storage conditions in the Suez Canal climate zone.

2. Key Challenges

  • Heat stress: Summer temperatures exceed 40°C in Upper Egypt, impacting feed intake, gut integrity, and growth rates in broilers and layers.
  • Mycotoxin risk: Corn and soymeal imported from Black Sea and South American origins frequently exceed safe thresholds — systematic control at intake is essential.
  • Feed hygiene: High ambient humidity in the Nile Delta accelerates mold growth in stored ingredients and finished feed.
  • Water salinity: Groundwater salinity in some regions compounds nutritional stress in poultry and ruminant operations.
  • Distribution complexity: Multiple intermediary layers between manufacturers and farms require strong technical support to ensure correct product application.

3. Our Approach in Egypt
