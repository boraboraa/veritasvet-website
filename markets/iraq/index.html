Iraq Market

1. Market Overview

Iraq's poultry and livestock sector has experienced significant growth over the past decade, driven by increasing domestic demand for protein and government programs supporting local food production. The country produces hundreds of millions of birds annually, with the sector concentrated around major urban centres including Baghdad, Erbil, Basra, and Mosul. Feed represents the dominant production cost for Iraqi livestock operations, and quality feed additives remain a critical input for maintaining productivity in challenging environmental and logistical conditions. VeritasVet addresses this need through a network of regional distribution partners who provide technical support and reliable product supply across all Iraqi governorates.

2. Key Challenges

  • Infrastructure variability: Operating conditions vary significantly between governorates, with storage capacity, cold chain availability, and distribution logistics presenting ongoing challenges for feed quality maintenance.
  • Feed ingredient sourcing: Iraq relies heavily on imported feed ingredients — corn, soybean meal, and mineral premixes — introducing variability in nutritional composition and mycotoxin risk profiles that require systematic management at mill level.
  • Heat stress: Summer temperatures frequently exceed 45°C in southern Iraq, placing extreme pressure on poultry gut health, feed intake, and water balance in both commercial and smallholder operations.
  • Disease pressure: High-density poultry operations near urban centres face elevated biosecurity challenges, increasing the economic value of preventive nutritional support through feed additives.
  • Access to technical knowledge: Many Iraqi feed operators and farm managers have limited access to specialized nutritional consultation, making distributor technical support a key differentiator in the market.

3. VeritasVet Approach in Iraq

VeritasVet works with Iraqi distribution partners who understand local operating conditions and can provide hands-on technical support to feed mills and farms across the country's diverse geography.

Our approach focuses on practical additive programs that address the most common productivity bottlenecks in Iraqi poultry and livestock operations: mycotoxin risk from imported ingredients, gut health maintenance during heat stress periods, and feed hygiene management in variable storage conditions.

We support our Iraqi partners with product training, technical sales materials, dosage protocols adapted to local feed matrices, and responsive account management to ensure consistent supply and support continuity.
