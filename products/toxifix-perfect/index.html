ToxyFix Perfect

Your Comprehensive Solution for Mycotoxin Detoxification and Organ Recovery

ToxyFix Perfect is a synergistic blend of specifically selected binders, enzymatic biotransformators, and botanicals designed to provide maximum protection against mycotoxin contamination. This advanced formulation features multi-layered mineral components and yeast sources that effectively adsorb, bind, and inactivate both polar and non-polar mycotoxins. Registered by EFSA (category 1, functional group m, EU No. 1060/2013).

Frequently Asked Questions

How does ToxyFix Perfect differ from standard mycotoxin binders?

ToxyFix Perfect combines multi-layered mineral binders with enzymatic biotransformators and botanicals, providing a multi-mechanism approach that targets both polar and non-polar mycotoxins. Unlike standard clay binders, it also includes Saccharomyces cerevisiae enzymes that chemically transform mycotoxins into non-toxic metabolites.

Which mycotoxins does ToxyFix Perfect target in ruminant feed?

ToxyFix Perfect targets aflatoxins (B1, B2, G1, G2), Ochratoxin A, T-2 Toxin, Zearalenone, and Deoxynivalenol (DON) in ruminant feed. The enzymatic biotransformation component is particularly effective for DON, which is not well-adsorbed by standard clay binders alone.

What inclusion rate is recommended for ToxyFix Perfect in high-risk feed?

For high-risk feed scenarios with known multi-mycotoxin contamination, inclusion rates of 2–3 kg/tonne are recommended. For routine prevention, 1–2 kg/tonne is typically sufficient. Adjust based on raw material mycotoxin testing results and regional risk assessment.

Can ToxyFix Perfect be used alongside other feed additives?

Yes, ToxyFix Perfect is compatible with most standard feed additives including vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and common antioxidants. It can be used alongside enzymes, probiotics, and acidifiers. A brief mixing trial is recommended when using in combination with novel feed additives for the first time.

Is ToxyFix Perfect effective against emerging mycotoxins like enniatins?

Yes, the combined binder and enzymatic approach in ToxyFix Perfect provides broader coverage than clay-only products. While enniatins are considered emerging mycotoxins with limited standard binder efficacy, the yeast-based biotransformation component helps reduce their toxicity.
