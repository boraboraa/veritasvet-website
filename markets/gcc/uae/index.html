Key Challenges

  • Premium quality standards: UAE food safety regulations require full technical documentation, batch traceability, and regulatory compliance on all additives.
  • Extreme heat and humidity: Year-round thermal pressure in Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and Northern Emirates demands continuous heat stress nutritional support.
  • Dairy and ruminant intensity: Large-scale dairy complexes require consistent micronutrient delivery, rumen stability, and metabolic health support at scale.
  • Re-export market reach: Products must meet the documentation and compliance requirements of multiple GCC end markets.
