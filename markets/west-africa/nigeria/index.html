Key Feed Challenges in Nigeria

🦠

High Mycotoxin Risk in Imported Grains

Imported Brazilian corn and Argentine soymeal regularly test above safe thresholds for aflatoxin B1, fumonisins, and DON. Multi-mycotoxin binder protocols at mill intake are critical.

💱

Unstable Feed Cost Structure

Naira depreciation against the dollar drives feed ingredient costs up unpredictably. Feed mills need cost-effective additive solutions that maximize ROI per tonne of feed.

🌡️

Heat Stress Impact on Poultry Performance

Extreme heat in the north reduces poultry feed intake and increases FCR above 2.0 during harmattan and summer months. Gut integrity support is essential.

❄️

Storage & Logistics Limitations

Inconsistent warehousing conditions accelerate mold growth and feed oxidation, particularly in the humid southern regions and during rainy seasons.

🏭

Inconsistent Feed Quality in Small Mills

Many smaller feed mills produce inconsistent feed quality due to poor ingredient testing and formulation variability — creating FCR gaps across farms.

🐔

High Feed Cost & FCR Inefficiency

With feed representing 65-75% of production cost, every 0.1 improvement in FCR translates directly to profitability. Nigeria's broiler FCR averages 1.8-2.2 — significant room for improvement.
