The Feed Safety Challenge

Why Feed Quality Is a Mill Performance Lever

Pellet Quality

Poor PDI and excessive fines increase feed waste at farm level, reduce feed intake in poultry, and damage your mill's reputation for quality. Pellet binders deliver measurable PDI improvements from day one.

Oxidative Rancidity

Unprotected fats and oils in feed oxidize during storage, producing rancid off-flavors that suppress feed intake and degrade fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K). Antioxidant blends stabilize feed fats and extend usable shelf life, especially in warm-climate storage.

Feed Hygiene

Mold growth in stored feed produces mycotoxins and reduces palatability. Mold control agents and acidifiers protect feed hygiene throughout the supply chain.

Storage Stability

Corn and soy frequently contain 3–5 mycotoxins simultaneously. Single-mode binders leave co-occurring toxins uncontrolled — requiring multi-spectrum solutions.
