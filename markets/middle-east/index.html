Middle East Market Solutions

VeritasVet supports feed mills, integrators, and distribution partners in the Middle East with dependable, high-performance additive programs tailored to heat, biosecurity pressure, and production intensity.

1. Market Overview

Middle East animal production systems demand high output consistency under hot climates, intensive production cycles, and strict commercial performance targets. Poultry-dominant operations especially require tightly controlled nutrition and feed hygiene to protect conversion, uniformity, and farm profitability.

VeritasVet works with technical and commercial teams to implement additive strategies that maintain biological performance while improving water and feed efficiency in demanding operating conditions.

2. Key Challenges

  • Heat stress pressure: Elevated ambient temperatures challenge intake, gut integrity, and metabolic efficiency, especially in fast-growth production models.
  • Performance intensity: Integrators require consistent conversion and output with minimal variance between cycles, houses, and farms.
  • Biosecurity demands: High stocking densities and disease-risk sensitivity increase the value of preventive nutritional support and feed hygiene control.
  • Water and feed efficiency: Limited resource efficiency margins require better nutrient utilization and lower avoidable losses in the full feeding chain.
  • Supply reliability pressure: Programs must remain stable despite shifts in logistics routes, import timelines, and regional operating complexity.

3. VeritasVet Approach

Our approach combines science-based additive design with operational discipline: matrix-aware formulation support, contamination-risk mitigation, species-tailored gut and performance support, and monitoring plans linked to measurable KPIs.

VeritasVet remains a stable and committed partner even in complex and uncertain environments, ensuring continuity of supply, technical support, and consistent product performance despite regional tensions or logistical challenges.

We support technical teams with clear implementation protocols, dosage logic by feed type, and responsive troubleshooting to preserve continuity across production cycles.
