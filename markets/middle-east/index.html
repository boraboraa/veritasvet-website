Middle East at a Glance

Dynamic Growth

One of the world's most dynamic feed additive markets — driven by rapid poultry sector expansion and GCC food security investment

Import Dependent

Majority of feed ingredients imported from multiple origins — introducing mycotoxin risk variability requiring systematic management

Extreme Climate

Year-round heat and humidity across most of the region creating persistent thermal stress pressure on livestock and poultry

Water Scarcity

Critical water shortages make feed efficiency per unit of animal product a primary operational and economic concern

Key Challenges

  • Heat stress pressure: Elevated ambient temperatures challenge intake, gut integrity, and metabolic efficiency across intensive production cycles.
  • Performance intensity: Integrators require consistent conversion and output with minimal variance between cycles, houses, and farms.
  • Biosecurity demands: High stocking densities and disease-risk sensitivity increase the value of preventive nutritional support and feed hygiene control.
  • Supply reliability: Programs must remain stable despite shifts in logistics routes, import timelines, and regional operating complexity.
