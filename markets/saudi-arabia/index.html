1. Market Overview

Saudi Arabia is the largest animal protein market in the GCC, with a poultry sector producing over 1 million tonnes annually. Vision 2030 has accelerated investment in domestic food production, including large-scale integrated poultry complexes.

Feed represents the largest single input cost. With limited domestic grain production, the sector relies heavily on imported ingredients — making mycotoxin control, feed hygiene, and nutritional efficiency critical operational priorities.

2. Key Challenges

  • Extreme heat stress: Summer conditions in central and eastern Saudi Arabia create year-round thermal pressure, requiring specialized nutritional support for intake, metabolism, and gut function.
  • Import dependency: Corn, barley, and soybean meal from Black Sea, South America, and Eastern Europe all carry documented mycotoxin risk profiles — requiring systematic testing and mitigation.
  • Water scarcity: Saudi Arabia's limited water resources make feed efficiency per unit of animal product a central economic and environmental concern.
  • Scale and biosecurity: Large integrated operations in the Riyadh, Eastern Province, and Jeddah corridors operate at high densities — biosecurity and disease prevention carry significant commercial value.
  • Premix precision: Intensive dairy and fast-growth broiler operations demand precise vitamin, mineral, and additive delivery systems.

3. Our Approach in Saudi Arabia

VeritasVet provides Saudi Arabian partners with high-quality feed additives, technical formulation support, and supply continuity backed by European manufacturing standards.

We work directly with feed mill quality teams to implement mycotoxin risk management protocols, feed hygiene SOPs, and species-specific performance programs. Our distribution partners benefit from dedicated technical training, marketing materials, and responsive account management.

For integrators, we offer co-development of additive specifications aligned with SASO quality standards and the specific protocols of large-scale complexes.
