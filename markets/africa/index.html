Africa at a Glance

Fast Growing

Africa's poultry sector is projected for significant growth — driven by rising protein demand, urbanization, and expanding middle-class consumption

Diverse Climates

From tropical West Africa to arid North Africa — distinct feed management challenges requiring locally adapted solutions

Import Risk

Imported feed ingredients face mycotoxin contamination issues requiring systematic risk assessment at mill level

Technical Gap

Limited access to specialized nutritional consultation makes distributor technical support a critical success factor

Key Challenges

  • Raw material variability: Frequent swings in ingredient digestibility, contamination pressure, and nutrient availability increase formulation uncertainty.
  • Climate and storage stress: Heat, humidity, and storage limitations accelerate oxidation, mold pressure, and nutrient loss across the feed chain.
  • Mycotoxin exposure: Multi-mycotoxin risk from local and imported ingredients can impair gut integrity, immunity, and feed efficiency if not managed proactively.
  • Logistics complexity: Long lead times and uneven warehousing require products with dependable shelf stability across the distribution chain.
