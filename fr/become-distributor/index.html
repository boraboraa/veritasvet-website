What we're looking for

We're building a network of long-term partners, not just moving product. Here's what matters to us.

Active in your market

You have an existing network in poultry, ruminant, or aquaculture — and the commercial infrastructure to reach feed mills and farms.

Technically capable

You have or can build the technical knowledge to support customers with product selection, dosage, and feed matrix guidance.

Committed to development

You're willing to invest in market development — field trials, farmer education, and consistent presence — not just order processing.

Regulatory alignment

You can manage or obtain the necessary product registrations, import licenses, or certifications required in your country.
