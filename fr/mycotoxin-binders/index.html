The Feed Safety Challenge

Why Mycotoxin Control Is Non-Negotiable

Performance Losses

Mycotoxines suppress feed intake, reduce FCR, impair weight gain and damage liver function — even at subclinical levels invisible to the eye.

Immune Suppression

Aflatoxin, fumonisin and T-2 toxin weaken immune response, increasing disease susceptibility and vaccination failures in poultry and ruminant flocks and herds.

Regulatory Risk

EU and MENA markets enforce strict mycotoxin action thresholds. Feed mills without documented control programs face rejected shipments and commercial liability.

Multi-Toxin Contamination

Corn and soy frequently contain 3–5 mycotoxins simultaneously. Single-mode binders leave co-occurring toxins uncontrolled — requiring multi-spectrum solutions.
