Key Challenges

  • Water scarcity: Jordan is among the world's most water-scarce nations — every unit of animal product must deliver maximum feed efficiency.
  • Import dependency: Corn, soybean meal, and mineral premixes are primarily imported, introducing mycotoxin variability requiring systematic management.
  • Heat stress: Summer temperatures of 35–40°C regularly challenge gut health, feed intake, and laying hen productivity.
  • Premix consistency: Dairy and small ruminant operations require reliable micronutrient delivery for metabolic function.
