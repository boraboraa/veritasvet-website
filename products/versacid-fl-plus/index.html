VersAcid FL Plus

Synergistic Fatty Acid Solution — Enhancing Animal Health and Performance

VERSACID FL Plus is a premium formulation combining short-chain fatty acids (formic acid, lactic acid, citric acid) with medium-chain fatty acids (capric acid and caprylic acid). This unique blend supports animal health, improves digestion, and enhances overall performance in livestock and poultry.

Key Benefits

  • Enhanced Gut Health — promotes balanced gut microbiota by inhibiting harmful bacteria
  • Improved Nutrient Absorption — increases feed digestibility for better nutrient utilization
  • Natural Antimicrobial Properties — formic and lactic acids reduce pathogens within the gastrointestinal tract
  • Boosts Immune Response — natural components contribute to greater resilience against diseases

How It Works

  • Formic Acid — strong antimicrobial inhibits harmful bacteria yeasts and molds
  • Lactic Acid — maintains healthy pH for optimal digestion
  • Citric Acid — natural preservative contributing to formulation stability
  • Capric and Caprylic Acids — strong antimicrobial effects and energy source promoting beneficial bacteria

Dosage

1–3 kg per ton of feed. Suitable for ruminants poultry and swine.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does VersAcid FL Plus protect against?

VersAcid FL Plus inhibits mold growth, bacterial contamination (including Salmonella and E. coli), and oxidative rancidity in feed and feed ingredients during storage and processing.

What is the difference between VersAcid FL Plus and VersAcid Liquid?

VersAcid FL Plus is a liquid fatty acid blend focused on feed preservation and hygiene, while VersAcid Liquid is a water-soluble multi-acid blend optimized for drinking water acidification and gut health.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between VersaAcid FL Plus and standard acidifiers?

VersaAcid FL Plus is a liquid blend of short and medium-chain fatty acids (primarily C6, C8, C10, and C12) combined with essential oils. Unlike standard acidifiers that primarily work in the upper GI tract, the fatty acid blend provides both gastric acidification and antimicrobial activity throughout the intestinal tract.

Can VersaAcid FL Plus be applied to feed as a top dressing?

Yes, VersaAcid FL Plus can be applied as a liquid top dressing to feed at 1–3 L/tonne of finished feed using standard liquid application equipment. This approach is particularly useful for poultry operations using mash feeds and provides both mold inhibition and gut health benefits.

Is VersaAcid FL Plus effective against mold in stored feed?

Yes, the medium-chain fatty acids (C8–C12) in VersaAcid FL Plus have proven antifungal activity against Aspergillus, Penicillium, and Fusarium species. Application at 2–3 L/tonne to finished feed significantly reduces mold growth and mycotoxin production during storage, especially in warm and humid conditions.

What species benefit most from VersaAcid FL Plus supplementation?

VersaAcid FL Plus provides the greatest benefits for poultry (broilers, layers, and breeders) where intestinal pathogen control and feed hygiene are critical. It is also highly effective for swine, particularly in nursery and grower phases where gut health challenges are most prevalent.

Does VersaAcid FL Plus have an effect on rumen fermentation?

VersaAcid FL Plus can be used in ruminants at lower inclusion rates (0.5–1 L/tonne of feed or 50–100 mL/head/day) to support rumen fermentation. The buffered formulation allows it to pass through the rumen while still providing antimicrobial benefits in the lower GI tract.
