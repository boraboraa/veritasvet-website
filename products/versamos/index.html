VersaMos

Prebiotic Supplement with MOS and β-Glucans — Enhancing Gut Health and Immunity

VERSAMOS is a high-quality prebiotic supplement formulated with Mannan-Oligosaccharides (MOS) and β-Glucans. Designed for all types of livestock, this innovative blend supports gut health, enhances immune response, and promotes overall animal well-being.

Key Benefits

  • Enhanced Gut Health — MOS derived from Saccharomyces cerevisiae promotes beneficial gut bacteria improves digestion and inhibits harmful pathogens
  • Immune Support — β-Glucans strengthen the immune system and enhance resistance to infections
  • Improved Feed Efficiency — enhances gut health and nutrient absorption improving feed conversion ratios
  • Stress Resistance — supports balanced gut microbiota during transport weaning and disease challenges
  • Versatile Application — suitable for poultry swine dairy cattle and beef cattle

Dosage

0.5–2 kg per ton of complete feed. Shelf life 24 months.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are MOS mannan-oligosaccharides?

MOS are prebiotic oligosaccharides derived from yeast cell walls that bind pathogenic bacteria (like Salmonella and E. coli) and support beneficial gut microbiota for improved gut health.

What species benefit most from VersaMos?

VersaMos is particularly beneficial for poultry and young livestock where gut health establishment and pathogen resistance are critical for performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does MOS stand for in VersaMos?

MOS stands for Mannan Oligosaccharides, which are derived from the cell walls of Saccharomyces cerevisiae yeast. VersaMos combines MOS with direct-fed microbials (DFM) including Bacillus and Lactobacillus strains to provide both gut health support and pathogen management.

Can VersaMos be used in pullet rearing diets?

Yes, VersaMos is highly recommended for pullet rearing diets from day one through to point of lay. The MOS component helps establish healthy gut microbiota in young birds, while the DFM component supports immune system development during the critical early growth phase.

How does VersaMos support pathogen management in poultry?

MOS contains mannose residues that competitively bind Type-1 fimbriae on Salmonella and E. coli, preventing their attachment to intestinal epithelial cells. The DFM component simultaneously produces bacteriocins and short-chain fatty acids that further inhibit pathogen colonization.

Is VersaMos compatible with acidifiers?

Yes, VersaMos is compatible with organic acidifiers such as formic acid, propionic acid, and citric acid. In fact, combining MOS with acidifiers can provide synergistic pathogen control as acidifiers reduce environmental pathogen load while MOS provides specific gut epithelial protection.

What gut health benefits does VersaMos provide in layers?

In laying hens, VersaMos supports consistent egg production by maintaining gut integrity and nutrient absorption efficiency. The DFM component helps stabilize the gut microbiome during stress periods, reducing the incidence of wet manure and improving feed conversion ratio (FCR) throughout the production cycle.
