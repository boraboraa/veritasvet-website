The Feed Safety Challenge

Why Intestinal Integrity Is Non-Negotiable

Reduced Feed Conversion

Poor gut integrity reduces nutrient absorption and feed efficiency — even at subclinical levels invisible to the eye.

Heat Stress Vulnerability

Heat stress and variable feed quality damage villi height, increasing disease susceptibility and vaccination failures in poultry and ruminant flocks and herds.

FCR Drag

Every 0.05 improvement in FCR represents significant cost savings. Feed mills without documented control programs face rejected shipments and commercial liability.

Variable Qualité de l'Aliment

Corn and soy frequently contain 3–5 mycotoxins simultaneously. Variable feed ingredients stress gut flora and villi integrity — requiring multi-spectrum solutions.
