GuarBind | Liant Alimentaire pour Granulés & Agent de Liage

Guar gum pellet binder reduces fines, improves PDI, and boosts feed conversion in poultry and livestock feed mills.

GUARBIND is a high-quality pellet binder formulated with natural guar gum, designed to enhance the durability and integrity of animal feed pellets. This innovative binding agent improves the physical properties of feed, ensuring your animal nutrition program is effective and efficient.

Avantages Clés

  • Improved Pellet Durability — reduces breakage during handling and transportation
  • Enhanced Feed Efficiency — minimizes dust loss preserving full nutritional value
  • Natural and Safe — derived from guar gum suitable for a variety of livestock species
  • Improves Palatability — encourages better feed intake
  • Versatile Application — suitable for ruminants poultry and aquaculture

Dosage & Storage

1–3 kg per ton of feed. Store in a cool dry place.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recommended inclusion rate for GuarBind?

Typical inclusion rates for GuarBind range from 0.5 to 2 kg per tonne of feed, depending on pellet durability requirements and feed formulation. Consult our technical team for specific recommendations.

Is GuarBind suitable for organic feed?

GuarBind is derived from natural guar gum and is suitable for conventional feed production. For organic certification, please consult your organic certification body.

Does GuarBind affect vitamin stability?

GuarBind is neutral to vitamins at recommended inclusion levels and does not negatively interact with fat-soluble vitamins in standard feed formulations.

What feed species is GuarBind most effective for?

GuarBind is effective across poultry, ruminant, and aquaculture feed applications, with particularly strong results in broiler feed where pellet hardness is critical for feed conversion efficiency.
