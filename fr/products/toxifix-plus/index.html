ToxyFix Plus

Éliminateur de Mycotoxines — Maximum Protection, Enzymatic Biotransformation and Immune Support

ToxyFix Plus is a synergistic blend of specifically selected binders and yeasts that stimulate enzymatic biotransformation. This advanced formulation features multi-layered mineral components and yeast sources that effectively adsorb, bind, and inactivate both polar and non-polar mycotoxins. Registered by EFSA (category 1, functional group m, EU No. 1060/2013).

Avantages Clés

  • Aflatoxins B1 B2 G1 G2 — 100% adsorption
  • Ochratoxin OTA — 95% deactivation
  • T-2 Toxin — 77% binding
  • Zearalenone ZEN — 95% binding
  • Enzymatic Biotransformation — Saccharomyces cerevisiae transforms toxic molecules into non-toxic metabolites
  • Immune System Support — positively impacts immune response and overall animal health
  • Antioxidant Protection — protects against cellular damage and lipid peroxidation

Frequently Asked Questions

What mycotoxins does ToxyFix Plus target?

ToxyFix Plus targets all major mycotoxins including aflatoxin, fumonisin, T-2 toxin, deoxynivalenol (DON), and zearalenone through its dual-mode binding technology.

What is the difference between ToxyFix and ToxyFix Plus?

ToxyFix Plus combines HSCAS bentonite clay with yeast cell wall beta-glucans for enhanced broad-spectrum binding, while ToxyFix uses HSCAS alone for cost-effective single-mode mycotoxin control.

What is the recommended inclusion rate?

Typical inclusion rates range from 1 to 3 kg per tonne of complete feed, depending on mycotoxin contamination levels and target species. Our technical team can provide specific formulation guidance.
