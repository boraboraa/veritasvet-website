Iraq at a Glance

Domestic protein demand driving sustained sector expansion and investment

Import-Dependent

Corn, soybean meal, and premixes primarily sourced through regional trade

Key Challenges

  • Infrastructure variability: Storage capacity, cold chain, and distribution logistics vary significantly between governorates.
  • Heat stress: Summer temperatures above 45°C in southern Iraq place extreme pressure on gut health and feed intake in poultry.
  • Feed ingredient sourcing: Imported ingredients introduce mycotoxin variability requiring systematic testing and mitigation protocols.
  • Disease pressure: High-density poultry operations near urban centres face elevated biosecurity and disease challenges.
