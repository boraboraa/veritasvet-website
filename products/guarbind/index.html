GuarBind

Guar Gum Pellet Binder — Enhancing Pellet Quality and Feed Efficiency

GUARBIND is a high-quality pellet binder formulated with natural guar gum, designed to enhance the durability and integrity of animal feed pellets. This innovative binding agent improves the physical properties of feed, ensuring your animal nutrition program is effective and efficient.

Key Benefits

  • Improved Pellet Durability — reduces breakage during handling and transportation
  • Enhanced Feed Efficiency — minimizes dust loss preserving full nutritional value
  • Natural and Safe — derived from guar gum suitable for a variety of livestock species
  • Improves Palatability — encourages better feed intake
  • Versatile Application — suitable for ruminants poultry and aquaculture

Dosage & Storage

1–3 kg per ton of feed. Store in a cool dry place.

Technical Specifications

Active IngredientGuar Gum (Cyamopsis tetragonoloba)
ApplicationPoultry, Ruminant, Aquaculture feed
Dosage Rate1–3 kg per ton of finished feed
SolubilityCold and hot water dispersible
Shelf Life24 months from manufacturing date
StorageCool, dry environment; sealed packaging

Application Areas

GuarBind is particularly effective in high-density pelleted feeds where physical integrity directly impacts intake efficiency and nutrient delivery. Recommended for:

  • Broiler and layer feeds where pellet fines increase segregation and reduce uniformity
  • Dairy concentrates where rumen-protected binding improves physical structure
  • Aquafeed pellets where water stability is critical for feed loss reduction

Implementation Guidance

For optimal results, GuarBind should be added at the mixer stage prior to pelleting. Pre-dispersion in warm water (40°C) is recommended for cold-water applications. Consult our technical team for species-specific formulation support.
