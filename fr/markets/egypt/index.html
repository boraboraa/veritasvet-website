The Reality on the Ground

Key Challenges

  • Heat stress — Summer temperatures exceed 40°C in Upper Egypt, reducing feed intake, damaging gut integrity, and slowing growth rates in broilers and layers across most production systems.
  • Mycotoxin risk — Corn and soymeal imported from Black Sea and South American origins frequently exceed safe thresholds. Systematic control at mill intake is non-negotiable.
  • Feed hygiene — High ambient humidity in the Nile Delta accelerates mold growth in stored ingredients and finished feed, requiring proactive mold control protocols.
  • Distribution complexity — Multiple intermediary layers between manufacturers and farms demand strong technical support networks to ensure correct product application at farm level.
