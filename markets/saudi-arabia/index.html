Key Challenges

  • Extreme heat stress: Year-round thermal pressure in central and eastern regions demands specialized nutritional support for intake and gut function.
  • Mycotoxin exposure: Imported feed ingredients carry documented multi-mycotoxin risk requiring systematic mitigation at mill level.
  • Water scarcity: Feed efficiency per unit of animal product is a central economic and environmental priority across all species.
  • Biosecurity intensity: Large-scale integrator operations require robust disease prevention through feed-based nutritional support.
