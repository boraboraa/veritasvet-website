Jordan Market

1. Market Overview

Jordan's animal production sector serves as a key hub for the Levant region, with poultry representing the largest segment. The country produces approximately 500 million day-old chicks annually, with a significant proportion supplied to Iraq and Syria through cross-border trade channels.

Jordan's feed industry operates under pressure from water scarcity, limited arable land, and a heavy dependence on imported feed ingredients — making feed efficiency and anti-mycotoxin programs commercial priorities.

2. Key Challenges

  • Water scarcity: Jordan is among the world's most water-scarce countries. Feed operations must maximize nutrient utilization per unit of water.
  • Import dependency: Corn, soybean meal, and mineral premixes are predominantly imported — introducing variability in mycotoxin profiles requiring systematic risk management.
  • Cross-border market dynamics: Jordan supplies Iraq and Syria, meaning feed quality must meet multiple end-market requirements with differing regulatory frameworks.
  • Heat stress: Summer temperatures exceed 35–40°C, impacting gut health, feed intake, and laying hen productivity.
  • Premix quality consistency: Dairy and small ruminant operations require consistent micronutrient delivery — vitamin E, selenium, and trace minerals for metabolic function.

3. Our Approach in Jordan

VeritasVet works with Jordanian feed businesses and distributors to implement targeted additive programs that address the country's specific resource constraints and market dynamics.

Our approach focuses on mycotoxin risk management protocols adapted to Jordan's imported ingredient profiles, feed hygiene systems compatible with local storage conditions, and premix optimization for the dairy and poultry sectors.
