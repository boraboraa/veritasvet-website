VeriVit Hy D3

Direct Source of Vitamin D3 — Ensuring Optimal Health with Calcidiol

VERIVIT Hy D3 Liquid is a groundbreaking nutritional supplement providing a unique source of Vitamin D3 known as calcidiol (25(OH)D3). Unlike traditional Vitamin D3 supplements which must first be processed by the liver, VERIVIT Hy D3 offers direct availability of calcidiol, facilitating optimal health and performance in livestock and poultry.

Key Benefits

  • Direct Availability — calcidiol bypasses the liver and is immediately available for utilization
  • Supports High Metabolic Activity — minimizes risk of deficiency during transition periods
  • Enhanced Calcium Absorption — promotes strong bones and prevents metabolic disorders
  • Improved Immune Function — supports immune regulation and resilience against infections

Dosage

Chickens for fattening 1L per 1000L drinking water / Turkeys for fattening 1L per 1000L drinking water / Other poultry 800ml per 1000L drinking water.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the advantage of calcidiol (25(OH)D3) over standard Vitamin D3?

Calcidiol is the intermediate form of Vitamin D3 that bypasses liver conversion, providing faster and more predictable blood calcium mobilization — especially valuable in stressed or fast-growing animals.

What species benefit most from VeriVit Hy D3?

VeriVit Hy D3 is particularly beneficial for fast-growing broilers, high-producing dairy cattle, and breeding animals with high calcium demands.
