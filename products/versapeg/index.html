VersaPeg

Non-Ionic Water Soluble Emulsifier — Enhancing Fat Digestibility and Energy Efficiency

VERSAPEG is a premium non-ionic emulsifier in powder form made with polyoxyethylene glycol-glyceryl ricinoleate (E484). Its high lipophilic nature makes it an excellent emulsifier for fats and oils in feed, enhancing fat digestibility, improving energy efficiency and optimizing overall feed performance for livestock, poultry and fish.

Key Benefits

  • Enhanced Fat Digestibility — E484 improves emulsification of fats for better breakdown and absorption
  • Boosted Nutrient Absorption — promotes absorption of fat-soluble vitamins A D E and K
  • Cost-Effective Nutrition — improves nutrient digestibility and energy efficiency while reducing feed costs
  • Optimal HLB Balance — ensures effective interaction with both water and fat enhancing feed formulation stability

Dosage

0.250–1 kg per ton of feed. Suitable for complete feeds supplements and premixes.

How VersaPeg Works

VersaPeg functions as a non-ionic emulsifier by reducing the surface tension between fat droplets and the aqueous phase in the digestive tract. Its HLB (Hydrophilic-Lipophilic Balance) value of approximately 14–15 makes it particularly effective at stabilizing oil-in-water emulsions common in poultry and ruminant feed digestion.

In poultry, where bile salt secretion can be limited — especially in young birds — VersaPeg compensates by physically emulsifying dietary fats, enabling lipase enzymes to access triglyceride molecules more efficiently. This results in measurably higher fat digestibility coefficients, particularly for saturated fats commonly used in broiler feed formulations.

For ruminants, VersaPeg improves fat utilization in the abomasum and small intestine, complementing the natural emulsification that occurs through bile secretion. In aquaculture feeds — where fish oil and plant oil blends are standard — VersaPeg ensures stable emulsion formation even at the low temperatures used in pellet cooling.

VersaPeg vs. Alternative Emulsifiers

Lecithin-based emulsifiers are widely used but vary significantly in phosphatidylcholine content and efficacy. VersaPeg (E484) offers consistent, batch-to-batch performance without the variability associated with natural lecithin sources. Compared to bile salt supplements, VersaPeg works synergistically with endogenous bile rather than replacing it, making it suitable for long-term inclusion at low dosages without disrupting natural digestive feedback loops.

Unlike surfactant-based products that can irritate the intestinal mucosa at high concentrations, VersaPeg's non-ionic nature makes it gentle on the gut epithelium while still delivering measurable improvements in fat digestion and energy utilization.

Species & Applications

Poultry

VersaPeg is particularly valuable in broiler and layer diets where high-fat feed formulations (5–8% supplemental fat) are common. Young chicks benefit from improved fat digestion during the critical first 21 days when bile salt production is still developing. In heat stress conditions, VersaPeg supports energy intake by compensating for reduced feed consumption while maintaining energy density in the diet.

Ruminants

For dairy cattle and beef feedlots, VersaPeg improves fat digestion in the post-ruminal tract, supporting milk yield and compositional quality. It is especially useful in transition cow rations where fat supplementation supports energy balance without compromising rumen fermentation.

Aquaculture

In fish and shrimp feeds, VersaPeg ensures stable oil-in-water emulsions during extrusion and reduces oil leakage during pellet storage. It supports efficient utilization of fish oil and plant oil blends, contributing to improved FCR (feed conversion ratio) in carnivorous species.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recommended inclusion rate for VersaPeg in poultry feed?

The recommended inclusion rate for VersaPeg (polysorbate 80) in poultry feed is 200–500 g per tonne of finished feed, depending on fat level and bird age. Broiler diets with high fat content (above 4%) typically use 300–500 g/tonne, while layer diets may require 200–300 g/tonne.

How does VersaPeg improve fat digestion in broilers?

VersaPeg is a non-ionic surfactant that emulsifies dietary fats into smaller micelles, increasing the surface area for lipase action. This improves fat digestibility by 5–15%, particularly for saturated fats and vegetable oils, leading to better energy utilization and improved FCR in broilers.

Is VersaPeg effective during heat stress periods?

Yes, VersaPeg is particularly beneficial during heat stress when endogenous bile salt secretion may be reduced and fat digestion is compromised. Including VersaPeg at 400–500 g/tonne during hot weather helps maintain fat digestibility and energy intake when feed consumption drops.

Does VersaPeg work in ruminant total mixed rations?

VersaPeg can be used in ruminant TMR at lower inclusion rates (100–200 g/tonne of dry matter) to improve fat digestion and prevent fat ball formation in the rumen. It is most useful in rations containing high levels of bypass fats or unsaturated fat sources.

What is the maximum inclusion level for VersaPeg in feed?

The maximum recommended inclusion level is 1 kg/tonne (0.1%) for poultry and swine feeds. Above this level, VersaPeg may reduce feed intake due to excessive emulsification in the upper GI tract. Always introduce gradually when first using the product.
