UAE Market

1. Market Overview

The UAE operates one of the most advanced food production sectors in the Gulf region. With Vision 2030 driving food security objectives across the GCC, the UAE has invested heavily in domestic poultry, dairy, and aquaculture production.

The UAE produces significant volumes of broiler meat and table eggs domestically, while serving as a major re-export and distribution hub for feed additives across the wider GCC region.

2. Key Challenges

  • Premium quality standards: UAE food safety regulations and integrator specifications demand additives with full technical documentation, batch traceability, and regulatory compliance.
  • Extreme heat and humidity: Summer conditions in Abu Dhabi, Dubai, and the Northern Emirates create year-round thermal pressure — heat stress management is a persistent operational priority.
  • Dairy and ruminant intensity: Large-scale dairy complexes require consistent micronutrient delivery, rumen stability solutions, and metabolic health support at scale.
  • Re-export market role: UAE distributors supply Oman, Qatar, Bahrain, and Kuwait — requiring product availability and regulatory documentation across multiple GCC markets.
  • Aquaculture growth: Commercial aquaculture for barramundi, seabream, and shrimp is expanding — requiring specialized feed additive solutions adapted to warm-water marine species.
