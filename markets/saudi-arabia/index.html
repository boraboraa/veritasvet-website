Saudi Arabia Market

1. Market Overview

Saudi Arabia is the largest animal protein market in the Gulf Cooperation Council, with a poultry sector producing over 1 million tonnes annually and ambitious targets for self-sufficiency. The Kingdom's Vision 2030 has accelerated investment in domestic food production, including large-scale integrated poultry complexes. Feed represents the single largest input cost in Saudi poultry and livestock operations. With limited domestic grain production, the sector relies heavily on imported feed ingredients — making mycotoxin control, feed hygiene, and nutritional efficiency critical operational priorities.

2. Key Challenges

  • Extreme heat stress: Summer conditions in central and eastern Saudi Arabia create year-round thermal pressure on livestock and poultry, requiring specialized nutritional support for intake, metabolism, and gut function.
  • Import dependency: Corn, barley, and soybean meal are predominantly imported from Black Sea countries, South America, and Eastern Europe — all origins with documented mycotoxin risk profiles requiring systematic testing and mitigation.
  • Water scarcity: Saudi Arabia's limited water resources make feed efficiency per unit of animal product a central economic and environmental concern, directly linking nutritional strategy to operational viability.
  • Scale and biosecurity: Large integrated operations in the Riyadh, Eastern Province, and Jeddah corridors operate at high stocking densities, where biosecurity and disease prevention through nutritional support carry significant commercial value.
  • Premix and additive precision: Intensive dairy and fast-growth broiler operations demand precise vitamin, mineral, and additive delivery systems, aligning with VeritasVet's premix and targeted additive capabilities.
